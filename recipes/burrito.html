Burrito Recipe

A burrito is a Mexican dish consisting of a soft tortilla filled with various ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • Flour tortillas
  • Cooked rice
  • Cooked beans
  • Cooked meat (chicken, beef, or pork)
  • Cheese
  • Salsa
  • Sour cream
  • Coleslaw or veggies of your choice

Steps:

  1. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  2. Place a tortilla on a flat surface and add a layer of rice, beans, and cooked meat.
  3. Add cheese, salsa, sour cream, and any additional toppings you like.
  4. Fold the sides of the tortilla over the filling, then roll it up tightly from the bottom to form a burrito.
  5. Serve immediately or wrap in foil for later consumption.
