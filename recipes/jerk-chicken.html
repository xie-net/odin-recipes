Jerk Chicken Recipe

Jerk Chicken is a spicy Caribbean dish made with marinated chicken and a blend of aromatic spices.

Ingredients:

  • 1 whole chicken cut in quarters
  • Marinade
  • 4 scallions
  • 1 yellow onion
  • Thyme
  • Garlic cloves
  • pimento seeds
  • 2-4 scotch bonnett peppers
  • Ginger
  • Oil
  • Chicken seasoning
  • Maggi All-purpose seasoning
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Walkerswood jerk seasoning

Steps:

  1. Prepare chicken, cut, pat dry and set aside.
  2. Cut ingredients onion, scallion, thyme, garlic, scotch bonnett and ginger.
  3. Combine above with seasonings listed.
  4. Apply generous amount of marinade to chicken.
  5. Marinate the chicken in the jerk seasoning for at least 2 hours.
  6. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  7. Grill the chicken for 6-8 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
