BBQ Chicken Recipe

BBQ Chicken is a classic American dish, but this is the Jamaican version. The chicken is marinated in a flavorful blend of spices and then grilled to perfection, resulting in a smoky and delicious dish.

Ingredients:

  • 1 whole chicken cut in quarters
  • Marinade
  • Escallions
  • Thyme
  • Garlic cloves
  • 2 scotch bonnett peppers
  • Ginger
  • Oil
  • Chicken seasoning
  • Maggi All-purpose seasoning
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Ketchup
  • Brown sugar
  • Barbecue sauce of your choice

Steps:

  1. Prepare chicken, cut, pat dry and set aside.
  2. Cut ingredients onion, scallion, thyme, garlic, scotch bonnett and ginger.
  3. Combine above with seasonings listed.
  4. Apply generous amount of marinade to chicken.
  5. Marinate the chicken in the bbq seasoning for at least 2 hours.
  6. Preheat the oven to medium-high heat.
  7. Roast the chicken for 6-8 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
  8. Let the chicken rest for a few minutes before serving.
